TERMINAL-DELIVERY PRIVACY

After completing the delivery verify:

* Driver coordinates are no longer returned to the Customer.
* Terminal route response contains no live driver location.
* Historical SSE/location-event replay cannot reveal driver coordinates after delivery completion.
* Customer cached tracking state is cleared correctly.
* Old driver marker/location is not retained after terminal state.
* Refreshing the completed delivery does not restore historical driver coordinates.

This is a security/privacy requirement.

5. SECURITY AND AUTHORIZATION

Perform safe authorization-negative tests.

Verify:

* Customer A cannot access Customer B’s private delivery information.
* Customer cannot modify another customer’s delivery.
* Driver cannot read or modify a delivery they are not authorized to access.
* Customer cannot call Driver-only APIs successfully.
* Customer cannot call Admin-only APIs successfully.
* Driver cannot call Admin-only APIs successfully.
* Unauthenticated callers cannot access protected delivery information.
* Protected write endpoints enforce authentication and authorization server-side.
* Terminal deliveries do not expose historical driver location.

Inspect browser-facing configuration/build output for accidental secret exposure.

Ensure private secrets are NOT bundled into browser code, including:

* Supabase service-role/secret keys
* private Google Maps server credentials
* Stripe secret keys
* Resend/email secret keys
* database credentials
* backend signing secrets
* other server-only environment variables

Browser-safe/public keys are not automatically defects; distinguish publishable/public credentials from private secrets correctly.
